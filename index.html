About

I am an Assistant Professor of Languages and Applied Linguistics at the University of California, Santa Cruz, where I direct the Bilingualism, Sociolinguistics, and Cognition (BiSoN) Lab. My research spans bilingualism, heritage and second language acquisition, psycholinguistics, sociolinguistics, and interactional linguistics, with a focus on how cultural identity and language experience shape bilingual language processing.

Research

Current and ongoing work in the lab examines code-switching, bilingual language processing, and how variation in language experience and cultural identity influences these processes across psycholinguistic and sociolinguistic frameworks. For lab projects, publications, and opportunities to get involved, visit the BiSoN Lab website.
